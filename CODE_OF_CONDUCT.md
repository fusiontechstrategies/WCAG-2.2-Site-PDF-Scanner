# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place for people with different backgrounds, roles, abilities, and levels of experience.

Accessibility work benefits from careful discussion and different perspectives. Everyone participating here is expected to help keep those discussions constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, evidence, and observable behavior.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar terms when practical.
- Treat lived experience and technical evidence seriously without presenting either as automatic proof of conformance.
- Protect privacy. Do not post credentials, private URLs, personal information, customer data, or confidential reports.
- Accept correction and update inaccurate claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Dismissive or demeaning comments about disability or assistive technology
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Knowingly unsafe guidance or misuse of the project against systems without authorization
- Spam or sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or reviews

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
