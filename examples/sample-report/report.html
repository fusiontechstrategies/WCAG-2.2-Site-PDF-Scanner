#2 Critical 3.3.2: Labels or Instructions Level A 508
1 instance Guided Fix ✗ Open ▾
⚠ What’s Wrong

Form control is not programmatically associated with an accessible label.

Selector: #email

👥 Why It Matters

Screen reader users will not know what data to enter into the form field.

🔧 How to Fix

Provide a `<label>` with a `for` attribute matching the input's `id`, wrap the input in a `<label>` tag, or use an `aria-label` attribute.

Read WCAG 3.3.2 guidance →
